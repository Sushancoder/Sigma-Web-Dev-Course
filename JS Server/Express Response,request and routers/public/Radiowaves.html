Introduction

Radio waves are a type of electromagnetic radiation with wavelengths in the electromagnetic spectrum longer than infrared light. Radio waves have frequencies from 300 GHz to as low as 3 kHz and corresponding wavelengths ranging from 1 millimeter to 100 kilometers.

Dangers

High levels of radio waves can potentially damage tissues in the body through heating effects. This is the basis of microwave ovens and certain medical applications such as diathermy. However, there is no strong evidence that low to moderate levels of radio wave exposure cause harmful effects.

Benefits

Some benefits of radio waves include wireless communication technologies like WiFi, mobile phones, radio broadcasting, and more. Radio waves are also used in applications like radar, GPS systems and medical imaging.
